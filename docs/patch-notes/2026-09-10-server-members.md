# Meet your server mates

- Open the member button beside Friends to search everyone on your server, whether or not you're friends.
- Choose “Show members below Friends” to keep a separate member list in the sidebar. Wisp remembers your choice.
- Add friends from the member list, room participants, or chat authors. Requests show a sent confirmation and can be accepted, declined, or cancelled.
- Incoming requests highlight the member button. Accepted friends can message each other in a new tile.

This release also strengthens the repository checks that keep private administration and account files out of published source.
