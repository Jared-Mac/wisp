# Phone alerts and Android testing

- Phone alerts now follow activity across your PC, including gaming. Messages still sync to every device.
- Automatic Away defaults to 30 minutes, with an adjustable timer and separate on/off setting. Manual Away allows phone alerts; expired or unavailable activity checks do too.
- Desktop startup and background updates preserve voice sessions on another device.
- **Android 0.2.0-test:** desktop-aware alerts, safer voice handoff, and improved invite links. Install directly from [the Android test page](https://wisp.you/android-test); no USB connection or Android Studio needed.
