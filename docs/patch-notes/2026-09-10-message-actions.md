# Replies, forwarding, and pinned messages

- Reply or forward from a message's three-dot menu. Replies include a quoted preview; forwarding lets you choose another chat or friend.
- Open pinned messages from the pin icon in any chat, including DMs. Server admins manage room and channel pins; both people can manage DM pins.
- Jump back to older messages from replies and pins. Forwarded images and files are copied securely into their new chat.

Update your client to read the new encrypted replies and forwards.
