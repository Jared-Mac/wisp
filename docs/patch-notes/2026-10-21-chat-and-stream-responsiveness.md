# Faster chat feedback and more reliable streams

- Text messages and replies appear immediately with a Sending indicator. Unconfirmed sends keep your draft and offer Retry for review.
- Fixed duplicate pasting when the same chat is open in multiple places, and messages flickering during background updates.
- Reduced unnecessary chat redraws and background work while voice or video is active.
- Fixed a startup race that could close a screen-share viewer before its video arrived.

The shared fixes are also being packaged for the Windows update feed; availability follows its build checks.
