# Account recovery and clearer errors

- Error banners now have a small X to dismiss them directly in the app and tray. Dismissing an error preserves your form entries and connection state.
- Add and verify a recovery email in **Settings → Profile**. **Forgot password?** sends a short-lived reset link from support@wisp.you. Password resets preserve your account, chats, and signed-in devices; keep your encryption recovery file too.
- **Android 0.2.1-test:** more reliable sign-in when a network advertises an unreachable address, plus clearer connection errors. Get it from [the Android test page](https://wisp.you/android-test) and install over the existing app to keep your settings.
