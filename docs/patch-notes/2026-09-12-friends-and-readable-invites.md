Friends now have their own view beside a compact Inbox, including incoming and
sent requests and private chats with friends outside your server. Server lists
show actual members and identify your friends. Desktop and tray remember their
view choices independently.

Server invitations use readable wisp.you links and expire within 12 hours. Paste
the full link, shortened link, or just its word-and-number code. Opening an invite
previews the server and offers Accept or Decline. Active word labels differ,
expired codes are never reused, and codes exclude inappropriate words and the
number sequences 69 and 666.

**Android test preview:** Saved sign-in, background message and knock alerts,
presence controls, message actions, profile settings, and two-chat layouts are
now in testing. The app also supports Wisp, personal, and server emoji, plus
readable invitation previews and sharing by code, link, or QR. Available as a
local test APK; a general Android download is not available yet.
