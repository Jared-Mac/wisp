# Soundboard and chat controls

- Made the soundboard smaller with a searchable grid, keyboard navigation, and volume and Stop controls above the sounds.
- Added a single Private preview toggle and kept uploads and removal in Manage sounds.
- Fixed room playback so you hear the effect through your selected speakers or headphones too. Volume and Stop apply to both local and room playback.
- Added Ctrl-drag anywhere in a tiled chat pane to move or swap it.
