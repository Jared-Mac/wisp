# Encrypted account backup

- Sign in on another supported device and restore your encryption identity and saved trust settings without transferring a recovery file. New accounts include encrypted backup; existing accounts can enable it in **Settings → Profile → Account backup** on a trusted device.
- Backup sync runs automatically, with a manual **Sync now** option. Interrupted sign-ins and account changes can be resumed without replacing your encryption keys.
- Secure password changes preserve backup access. After an email password reset, use an existing trusted device to restore access to the encrypted backup.
- **Android 0.3.0-test:** in-app update checks, clearer dismissible errors, and account recovery screens are available from [the Android test page](https://wisp.you/android-test). Android support for encrypted account backup will follow in a separate release.
