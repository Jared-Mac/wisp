# Simpler account update

- Existing accounts can finish their one-time update with their current password. Your password, friendships, encryption keys and chats stay the same.
- Wisp offers a compact confirmation in the app, with a Later option. The separate Account backup settings section is gone; advanced sync controls are under Privacy → Encryption details.
- New accounts continue to set everything up automatically. Interrupted updates and trusted-device recovery remain available.
- After confirming on your original desktop, you can sign in on the released Android 0.4 test app with that same password and restore your keys.
