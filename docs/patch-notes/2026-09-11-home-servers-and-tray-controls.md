# Home servers and a tidier tray

- Tray audio controls now stay in one compact row at the bottom, leaving more space for chat. Hover the room name to see who is connected.
- Choose one or more home servers. Homes stay at the top of the server dropdown; drag servers or use the arrows to arrange their order.
- Get optional desktop alerts when friends join rooms on your home servers, with controls for timing, cooldown, and sound. Clicking an alert opens the room chat.
- Soundboard menus now make hidden sounds easier to discover. Hover a sound for a separate private-listen button; microphone mute and deafen rules still apply.
