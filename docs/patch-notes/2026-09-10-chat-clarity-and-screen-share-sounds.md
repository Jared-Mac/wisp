# Clearer chats and screen-share cues

- Consecutive messages from the same person now share an avatar and use tighter spacing. Names and timestamps remain visible on every message.
- Add reactions directly from the message heading on hover or keyboard focus. Existing reactions stay visible, and invite cards no longer accept reactions.
- Screen shares now have distinct start and stop sounds in your current voice room. Customize or disable them under Notifications → Voice sounds.
