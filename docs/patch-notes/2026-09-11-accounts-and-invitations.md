# Accounts, invitations, and finding friends

- Create a Wisp account or sign in without an invitation. Friends and DMs work even when you haven't joined a server.
- Server invitations now use short HTTPS links and QR codes, with a preview, expiry, and revoke controls. Joining a server no longer automatically adds the inviter as a friend.
- Find friends by public username, accept or decline requests, and manage blocked accounts. Existing sign-in usernames stay private until you choose a public username in Profile settings.
- Joining an invitation can reuse your signed-in account. Leaving a server keeps your account, friends, and DMs. Neither action automatically starts voice, camera, or screen sharing.
