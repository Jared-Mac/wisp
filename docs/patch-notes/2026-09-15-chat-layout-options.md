# Choose your chat layout

- Appearance now offers **Grouped**, **Compact**, and **Soft groups** chat layouts.
  Grouped is the default; your selection is remembered in the app and tray.
- Grouped shares an avatar and header across consecutive messages. Compact puts
  names beside messages; Soft groups collects them in subtle cards.
- Message actions appear on hover or keyboard focus, keeping chats cleaner while
  reactions, replies, forwarding and pins remain easy to reach.
